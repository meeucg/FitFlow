# FitFlow

FitFlow is a job recommendation and interview preparation platform for freelance and tech specialists.

The project helps users avoid manually watching many job boards, freelance marketplaces, and Telegram channels. Instead, FitFlow collects postings, cleans and normalizes them, compares them with a user's professional profile, and shows the most relevant opportunities in one place.

Users can build their profile through an interview-style flow, keep their skills and interests structured, and receive a live recommendation feed tailored to their background. The goal is to make job discovery feel less like endless searching and more like a focused stream of opportunities that already fit.

FitFlow is designed around three main user needs:

- finding relevant freelance and tech projects faster;
- preparing a structured professional profile through guided interviews;
- receiving live recommendations as new matching opportunities appear.

At a high level, FitFlow combines a web application, authentication, AI-assisted profile and posting processing, and a recommendation engine that ranks normalized vacancies against user profile data.
